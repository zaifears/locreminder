Fixes a missed alarm. With two alarms set, the second could stay silent
for the whole journey and then ring on the way back past it. Two separate
faults each caused that, and both are fixed.

Vibration now works when the phone is on silent or Do Not Disturb. It was
being treated as an ordinary notification buzz and muted, which left the
phone playing the tone while sitting perfectly still in a pocket.

Location is now checked more often when you are travelling fast, and the
alarm area starts wider if you set an alarm while already moving. At
motorway speed the app could previously travel far enough between checks
to cross a small alarm area without noticing.

Messages appear at the bottom of the screen again instead of the middle,
and clear after a few seconds. Tapping the search bar now opens the
keyboard straight away.
