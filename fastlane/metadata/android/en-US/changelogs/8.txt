Corrects the About screen, which still described the app as using
Android's built-in geofencing after that was removed in 1.6.0.

The device-setup guidance now covers more manufacturers: Honor is listed
separately from Huawei since its menus differ, and Meizu, Asus, Lenovo,
ZTE and LeEco have been added. Phones that follow stock Android are now
named and told plainly that no extra setup is needed, instead of being
shown a generic warning.
