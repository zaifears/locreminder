Three fixes for alarms that could quietly fail to ring.

Arming an alarm while location was switched off left the app watching
nothing, while still saying it was watching. It now tells you, and starts
the moment you turn location on.

A rough position fix could set the alarm off kilometres early. Arrival is
now confirmed against how precise the fix actually is.

"Centre on my location" did nothing after a restart. It now asks for a
fresh position.
