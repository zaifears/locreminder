Three fixes for alarms that could quietly fail to ring.

Arming an alarm while location was switched off left the app watching
nothing at all, while still showing "Watching for your destination". It
now tells you, and starts working the moment you turn location on.

A rough position fix could set the alarm off kilometres early. Arrival
is now confirmed against how precise the fix actually is.

"Centre on my location" did nothing at all after a restart, because it
only read a position the phone had already cached. It now asks for a
fresh one, and says so if it can't get it. The blue dot also shows how
accurate it is, rather than implying it is exact.
