Fixes a crash. Zooming the map out quickly could leave it frozen and take
the app down with it. The map now stops at a whole-world view instead of
zooming out without limit, which is what caused it.

The blue from the app icon is now the app's colour everywhere, in both
light and dark themes: the alarm switches, the Add alarm button, the
About screen, the menu and the rest. It was always meant to be, but the
colour the app actually used was a muted version of it.

Also fixed: the circle no longer drifts away from the pin while you drag
the map, one unreadable saved alarm can no longer stop the app starting
at all, the alarm test now says so when it cannot start, and the
topographic map stays readable when you zoom right in.
