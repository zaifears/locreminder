Fixes a crash: zooming the map out quickly could freeze it and take the app
down. The map now stops at a whole-world view instead of zooming out without
limit.

The blue from the app icon is now the app's colour everywhere, in light and
dark themes.

Also fixed: the circle no longer drifts away from the pin while you drag the
map, one unreadable saved alarm can no longer stop the app starting, and the
topographic map stays readable when you zoom right in.
