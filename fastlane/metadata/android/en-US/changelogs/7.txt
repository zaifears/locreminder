LocReminder no longer uses Google Play Services at all. Location now runs
on Android's own platform APIs.

This means the app works on phones with no Google services: post-2019
Huawei and Honor devices, and de-Googled ROMs like LineageOS, /e/OS and
GrapheneOS. It also makes the app fully free software.

Detection is unchanged in practice — the foreground watcher was already
doing the work, and the geofencing that was removed had proved unreliable
on the very devices that need it most.
