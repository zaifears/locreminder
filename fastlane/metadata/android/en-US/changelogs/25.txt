Better advice for more phones. Around fifteen more manufacturers now get
step-by-step instructions for stopping the phone killing background
alarms, including TCL, Alcatel, HTC, Sharp, Amazon Fire tablets, the
MediaTek-based budget brands, and Walton, Symphony, Lava and Micromax.
Around forty-five brands are named now.

Phones the app did not recognise used to be told they were fine, which
hid the general advice that was the only help available to them. They see
it now.

Groundwork for other languages. Everything the app says now lives in one
file per language, so translating LocReminder means editing a single file
and nothing else. The app is still English only; this is the plumbing.
