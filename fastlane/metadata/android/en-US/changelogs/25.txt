Better advice for more phones. Around fifteen more manufacturers now get
step-by-step instructions for stopping the phone killing background alarms,
including TCL, Alcatel, HTC, Sharp, Amazon Fire tablets and the MediaTek
budget brands. Around forty-five brands are named now.

Phones the app did not recognise used to be told they were fine. They now
get the general advice instead.

Groundwork for other languages: everything the app says now lives in one
file per language.
