Alarms can now carry a task in your own words. The label used to be
whatever the place was called, so "Buy medicine" arrived as the name of
the pharmacy. Leave it blank and it still uses the place name.

Alarms can also repeat: once, every day, or on the days you choose. A
repeating alarm stays armed after it rings, so a commute or a standing
errand is set once rather than every morning. It rings once on each day
it is set for.

The About screen now links to GitHub only.
